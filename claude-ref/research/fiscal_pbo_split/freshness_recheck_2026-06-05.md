## Item 3 — FY2025-26 issuance outturn: **FRESH (still a plan; no outturn exists)**

No actual FY2025-26 issuance outturn has been published. Plate-6's framing of $612B as
a **planned** record is correct and cannot yet be either upgraded to observed fact or
contradicted.

- The most recent **Debt Management Report is 2024-2025** (FY ended March 31, 2025;
  total market debt $1,481.2bn). There is **no DMR 2025-26** — that report publishes in
  the fall, ~6 months after FY close. So no authoritative annual issuance outturn for
  FY2025-26 exists as of 2026-06-05.
- The $612B figure originates in the **2025-26 Debt Management Strategy** (total
  borrowings $612B domestic + $11B foreign), confirmed as a planned/projected number,
  with the DMS itself noting "actual borrowings... may be higher or lower than expected."
- BoC's gross-new-issues statistical table (formerly F4–F10) carries monthly data
  through April 2026, but it is a securities-statistics table, not an authoritative
  "record annual issuance" outturn statement. It does not warrant upgrading "planned
  record" to "observed record."

Plate-6's title ("Bonds, not bills, pushed issuance to a planned record") and body
("Issuance was set to reach a record $612 billion under the FY2025-26 borrowing plan")
correctly mark this as a plan. **Keep the "planned" qualifier** — do not drop it to
"record" until the DMR 2025-26 outturn lands. FRESH as written.
