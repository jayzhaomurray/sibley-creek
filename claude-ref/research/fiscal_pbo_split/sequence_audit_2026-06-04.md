## A. HEDS-ONLY read (01 → 06)

Read in succession with nothing else on the page:

> 01 Ottawa is balancing the operating books and borrowing the rest for capital.
> 02 Ottawa balances its operating books only on its own definition of capital.
> 03 The deficit narrows from the spending side; revenue holds.
> 04 Ottawa and the PBO both see the debt ratio holding flat.
> 05 The debt ratio is flat, but the bill to carry it keeps rising.
> 06 The bond program, not bills, drives issuance to a record near $612 billion.

| seam | verdict | why |
|------|---------|-----|
| 1→2 | CLEAN | 01 states the plan ("balance operating, borrow the rest"); 02 turns the same clause against it ("only on its own definition"). Pure setup→catch. The repeated "balances its operating books" is the hinge, not a stutter. |
| 2→3 | **BROKEN** | 02 is about *who defines capital / can the anchor be verified*. 03 opens "The deficit narrows from the spending side" — a fresh subject (the composition of consolidation) with no bridge from the verification dispute. Reading heds-only, 03 lands as a topic change. Jay's flagged suspect, confirmed. |
| 3→4 | CLEAN | 03 is about the *flow* (deficit narrowing, how); 04 steps to the *stock* (debt ratio) and adds the agreement note. Natural flow→stock progression; "both see ... flat" also rhymes against 02's dispute, quietly signalling "this part isn't contested." |
| 4→5 | CLEAN | 05 opens by quoting 04's own finding ("The debt ratio is flat, but...") and pivots to the bill. Textbook concession→turn. Strongest seam on the page. |
| 5→6 | SOFT | 05 ends on the rising *carrying cost*; 06 jumps to the *issuance program / bond vs bills*. The logical bridge (a bigger bill + bigger deficits means more to fund in the market) is real but unstated in the heds. Reads as related-but-adjacent, not driven. The "$612 billion" record lands as a new fact rather than the consequence of 05. |

Heds-only story currently reads: plan → catch → [jump] → stock → bill → [adjacent] market. Two weak seams (2→3 broken, 5→6 soft); the spine 01-02 and 04-05 is excellent.

## FIX LIST (minimal; one surface per seam where possible)

Principle: gate-passed copy moves as little as possible. Each fix names the
surface that changes and the surface that stays.

### Fix 1 — seam 2→3 (BROKEN), heds AND blurbs

The break is that 03 opens a *new analytic frame* ("spending story, not revenue")
with no carry from 02's verification dispute. The minimal bridge: have 03 land
*off the deficit itself* (the thing 01-02 established persists) rather than
opening cold on composition. The deficit is the through-line; 03 should pick it
up explicitly before splitting it spending-vs-revenue.

**Surface that changes: plate-03 blurb OPENER only. Hed and chart stay.**

- Current opener: "The deficit era is a spending story, not a revenue one."
- Proposed opener: "Whatever the definition fight, the deficit itself is a spending story, not a revenue one."

Rationale: "Whatever the definition fight" is a six-word bridge that reaches back
to 02 (the catch) and signals "set the dispute aside; here is what the deficit is
made of." It does NOT reference a chart or plate ("as the previous chart shows"
stays banned). It keeps three-surface standalone — the sentence still asserts its
own claim. Everything after sentence 1 is untouched.

**Heds:** the 03 hed ("The deficit narrows from the spending side; revenue holds")
is left AS-IS. Once the blurb opener bridges, the hed reads as the section
heading for "here's how the deficit narrows" — and heds-only, the 02→03 jump is
softened because 02's hed ("on its own definition of capital") and 03's ("narrows
from the spending side") now both clearly orbit the single deficit, not two
topics. If Jay wants the heds-only read airtight, optional alternate 03 hed:

- Optional: "Set the dispute aside: the deficit narrows from spending, not revenue."
- (12 words; explicitly bridges 02 in heds-only. Costs the clean "; revenue holds"
  cadence. RECOMMEND keeping current hed + fixing only the blurb opener — lighter
  touch, and the blurb fix carries the seam.)

### Fix 2 — seam 4→5 (banned page-internal reference), blurb 05 opener — MANDATORY

This is a defect, not a preference: "Plate-4's flat debt ratio" is a banned
page-internal reference (writing-style three-surface rule; "as the previous
chart shows" family). Must change regardless of sequence verdict.

**Surface that changes: plate-05 blurb OPENER (first sentence). Hed/chart stay.**

- Current draft opener: "Plate-4's flat debt ratio does not mean a flat bill."
- Proposed opener: "A flat debt ratio does not mean a flat bill."

Rationale: drop the "Plate-4's" possessive. "A flat debt ratio" still hands off
cleanly from 04 (which just established the flat ratio) via claim-ordering, not
cross-reference — exactly the mechanism Jay wants carrying the sequence. Stands
alone on the three-surface test ("a flat debt ratio" is a general macro premise
the chart then qualifies). The 05 hed already does this correctly ("The debt
ratio is flat, but...") with no reference — the blurb just needs to match that
discipline. Note: this fix is already half-anticipated in the draft, which marks
the opener as DRAFT.

### Fix 3 — seam 5→6 (SOFT), blurb 06 OPENER only

The bill (05) and the issuance program (06) are causally linked (more debt +
bigger deficits = more to fund) but the link is unstated, so 06 reads adjacent.
Minimal bridge: have 06 open by naming WHY the program is at a record — the same
deficits/refinancing 05's bill implies — before pivoting to the bond-vs-bills
finding.

**Surface that changes: plate-06 blurb OPENER only. Hed and chart stay.**

- Current opener: "The record is a bond-program story."
- Proposed opener: "Funding that rising bill sends issuance to a record — and the record is a bond-program story."

Rationale: "Funding that rising bill" reaches back to 05's land ("debt growing
faster than revenue" / the carrying cost) without a chart reference; "the record
is a bond-program story" is the original opener preserved verbatim as the second
clause, so the blurb's own argument and the rest of the paragraph are untouched.
Three-surface standalone holds — "funding [a] rising bill" is self-explanatory
even cold. The sentence 06-body already names "larger deficits and a heavier
refinancing load," so the new opener is consistent with what follows, not a
bolt-on.

Lighter alternate (if Jay wants 06 to keep its crisp 5-word opener): leave 06
opener as-is and instead add ONE land-clause to 05:

- 05 current land: "...debt is still growing faster than the revenue meant to service it."
- 05 alt land: "...debt is still growing faster than the revenue meant to service it — and that gap is what the market has to fund."

(Pushes the bridge onto 05's close instead of 06's open. Costs 05 a tighter
ending. RECOMMEND the 06-opener fix over this — the bridge belongs on the side
that changes register, i.e. 06, and a forward-looking land on 05 risks reading
as a punt to the next chart.)

### Heds-only 5→6 (SOFT)

Leaving both heds as-is; the blurb-06-opener bridge (Fix 3) carries the seam in
the blurbs read. For the heds-only read, the 05→06 jump is acceptable as a
"so what funds it?" beat — the page's final move from burden to market is a
legitimate register shift and a hed is allowed to introduce the new subject
(issuance) as the closing beat. No hed change proposed. If Jay disagrees, the
only clean hed-side fix would re-open 06 on the deficit/funding need, which
costs the strong "$612 billion record" payload. RECOMMEND no hed change.

## LEDE (synthesis layer) — does it need to change?

Current lede (sections.ts fiscal blurb.body), three sentences:
1. stance mildly expansionary, in deficit through decade; gap narrows because
   spending held below GDP growth, not revenue (← covers beats 01, 03)
2. operating surplus by 2028-29, funded by record bond issuance; PBO can't
   verify because definitions undisclosed (← covers beats 02, 06)
3. debt holds ~41% (DoF) / 42.5% (PBO), two tracks barely diverge (← covers beat 04)

**The lede does NOT cover the NEW beat 05 (the carrying cost / the bill).** It
currently jumps from the debt *ratio* (sentence 3, beat 04) and never lands the
*bill rises anyway* turn — which is now a full plate and arguably the page's
sharpest forward claim. This is a genuine gap, not a seam nicety.

**RECOMMEND: add the carrying-cost beat to the lede.** Minimal insertion —
extend sentence 3 (or add a short fourth clause) so the synthesis layer carries
all six beats. Proposed (extends sentence 3):

- Current: "Federal debt holds roughly flat near 41% of GDP on the government's
  plan—42.5% on the watchdog's—two tracks that barely diverge."
- Proposed: "Federal debt holds roughly flat near 41% of GDP on the government's
  plan—42.5% on the watchdog's—but the cost of carrying it climbs anyway, as
  debt outgrows the revenue meant to service it."

Rationale: a flat ratio + rising carrying cost is the 04→05 turn that is the
spine of the back half; the lede should carry it. This drops the "two tracks
barely diverge" tail — acceptable, because that detail is plate-04's job and the
lede is tighter without it. **This is a framing change → needs Jay's veto**, and
it introduces a synthesis-level claim ("cost of carrying it climbs") that must
re-clear gates against the same PBO/Fiscal-Monitor cards plate-05 binds to
(both currently in _pending/). If Jay prefers zero new lede dependencies until
those cards clear, hold the lede edit and ship the plate fixes alone — but flag
that the lede then under-represents the six-beat page.
