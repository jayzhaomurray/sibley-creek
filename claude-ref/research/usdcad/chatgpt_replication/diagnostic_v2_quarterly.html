Methodology summary

The pipeline uses a chronological 80/20 split. Filtering, imputation, robust scaling, sign determination, selection, and thresholds are fit on training only. Embedded selection combines Elastic Net, shadow-variable Boruta, and clustered MDA using directional hit-rate decrease. The product score is an equal-weight signed robust-z composite.

Limitations and failure modes

Forward returns overlap, reducing independent observations. Feature release lags are inherited from the provided processed dataset. The CV remains post-selection for final selected variables, so the hold-out remains the key evidence. Extreme readings can fail under regime shifts or if the training score distribution no longer calibrates live score magnitudes.
