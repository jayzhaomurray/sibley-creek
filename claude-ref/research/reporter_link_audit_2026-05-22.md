- **Highest-value target: The Hub (Sean Speer / Stuart Thomson).** Only outlet in the audit that consistently wraps independent research orgs in follow-links. One Hub citation likely resolves Sibley Creek's indexing problem inside 2 weeks.

The most prestigious Canadian macro outlet is an SEO dead end.

## What this means for Sibley Creek

1. **Tier-1 prestige outlets (Bloomberg, WSJ, Reuters, Globe, CBC) do not hyperlink independent research shops.** Globe citation is reputational gold and SEO zero. Pitch them for name-recognition; do not expect them to fix the indexing problem.
2. **The Hub is the SEO-feasible target.** Sample shows 6+ external orgs hyperlinked in one DeepDive. Pitch Sean Speer or Stuart Thomson with a DeepDive proposal. Methodology PDF as anchor.
3. **Substack peers (Moffatt, Tombe) are the second SEO path.** Lateral citation between indie research substacks is the most reliable backlink type.
4. **Financial Post needs a focused direct audit** — historically most link-tolerant of majors but blocked by search index quirks this session.
5. **Bank-economist channels (Harapyn-style interviews) are a dead end for the link goal.** Watch position: appear as guest, not as the cited shop.

## Method + caveats

- WebFetch on rendered HTML, extractor instructed to flag `<a href>` wraps and `rel="nofollow"` on each external research source.
- Bloomberg.com, cbc.ca, reuters.com, thelogic.co return 403/WAF — patterns inferred from peer outlets + republication mirrors.
- 8 articles successfully fetched and inspected. Single-article inference for any one byline is thin; recommend re-sampling each outlet quarterly as pitches go out.
- "Follow-link" status is `rel` absence — WebFetch abstracts the raw HTML so a View-Source check before pitching to The Hub is advised to confirm no nofollow at the template level.
