## Pitch topics (folded in from the researcher pass, 2026-06-02)

### Lead 3 (pitch these first)

**1. Who Actually Banks the TFSA?** — Format: Standard Chart Pack (~$16k) or Flagship
(~$32k if paired with RRSP). Hook: the TFSA is sold as everyone's shelter — whose tax
bill does it actually cut, and how big is the revenue forgone 16 years in? Key exhibit:
share of total TFSA assets + tax benefit held by the top quintile vs. the bottom two,
plus the rising federal cost line. Sources: CRA TFSA statistics, Finance tax-expenditure
report, StatCan SFS. Why CTO: direct methodological sequel to "Shelter vs. Tax Shelter";
pro-equality/simplicity; defensible to Milligan.

**2. The Capital Gains U-Turn** — Format: Flagship (~$32k) or Standard Pack (~$16k).
Hook: Ottawa proposed raising the inclusion rate in 2024, deferred, then abandoned it in
2025 — what was at stake, who'd have paid, what does the whipsaw say about taxing
capital? Key exhibit: distribution of realized gains by income group (top 1%/0.1% share)
vs. PBO/Finance revenue estimates + one-time vs. recurring split. Sources: CRA Final
Statistics, PBO costing, Budget 2024/2025. Why CTO: the defining capital-tax episode of
the decade; pro-growth vs. pro-equality tension.

**3. Bracket Creep, Reversed?** — Format: Mini/Standard Pack (~$9–16k). Hook: inflation
pushed Canadians up brackets for 3 years; the lowest federal rate was cut in 2025 — net
it out, who pays a different effective rate than 2021? Key exhibit: federal effective-rate
curve 2021 vs. 2026, decomposed into indexation / rate cut / threshold changes. Sources:
CRA indexation factors + bracket history, Finance budget measures, StatCan CPI. Why CTO:
pro-simplicity in its purest form; hits Heather's "government numbers are painful to
assemble" pain point dead-on; fast, low-friction, low microdata risk.

### Next 5

**4. Tax Expenditures vs. Direct Spending** — Standard Pack (~$16k) or Core Data
Workbench (~$16–24k). The $200B+/yr spent through the tax code with less scrutiny than
program spending — which grow fastest, deliver least per dollar. Natural Workbench
companion (refreshable tracker keyed to the annual Finance release). Sources: Finance
tax-expenditure report, PBO.

**5. Where the Money Lives — Provincial Tax-Burden Map** — Mini/Standard Pack (~$9–16k).
All-in combined federal-provincial burden across 10 provinces at $50k/$100k/$250k/$1M,
plus sales-tax design. Sources: provincial budget tables, CRA schedules, StatCan.

**6. The GST/HST Question** — Standard Pack (~$16k). Where consumption-tax burden actually
lands gross vs. net of the GST credit; single-year-income vs. lifetime-consumption basis.
Sources: StatCan Survey of Household Spending, CRA GST/HST credit stats.

**7. The Small-Business Deduction — Break or Loophole?** — Flagship (~$32k) or Standard
Pack (~$16k). Who claims the SBD/CCPC benefit now — Main Street employers or incorporated
professionals? Sources: CRA corporation statistics, Finance, PBO. NOTE: real aggregation
risk in public CRA T2 tables — scope to public data, flag limits, don't promise
regression-grade detail.

**8. The Carbon-Tax Aftermath** — Data-Backed Insight (~$3.5k) scaling to Mini Pack
(~$9k). Net rebate-minus-cost position by decile/province under the program's final year,
and the fiscal hole now both levy and rebate are gone. Sources: PBO distributional
analyses, Finance, StatCan. Low-friction entry that scales.
