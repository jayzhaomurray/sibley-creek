# R2 Research Cell Workflow

Use this when the project requires judgment, not just execution.

## When To Use

- deep-dive thesis development;
- flagship argument framing;
- ambiguous macro interpretation;
- central chart selection;
- major editorial stance;
- analysis with plausible alternative explanations.

## Roles

- Coordinator: classifies the task, writes the brief, assigns work, and writes the referee memo.
- Builder: develops the argument or analysis.
- Red team: tries to falsify, narrow, or reframe the argument.
- Jay: decides only on the synthesized recommendation or explicit unresolved questions.

## Required Artifacts

- `task_brief.md`
- claim cards for central claims
- builder memo or draft
- `red_team_audit.md`
- `referee_memo.md`

## Process

1. Coordinator writes the task brief.
2. Builder develops the best affirmative case.
3. Red team reviews for factual gaps, overclaiming, missing alternatives, weak sources, and chart misuse.
4. Coordinator reconciles the two views.
5. Referee memo states what survives, what should be cut, and what requires more evidence.

## Output Standard

The final synthesis must be usable by Jay without reading raw model transcripts.

It must answer:

- what we can safely claim;
- what we cannot claim;
- what evidence is missing;
- what the next decision is.
