# public/about/sleeping-giant.jpg - source and license

**Subject.** The Sleeping Giant landform on the Sibley Peninsula in
Lake Superior, viewed from the Thunder Bay waterfront. Three flat-
topped diabase mesas (head, chin, body) separated by two erosion
notches (throat, Adam's apple); the silhouette reads as a reclining
figure. A sailboat and the Thunder Bay marina sit in the foreground,
the lake mid-band, the Giant on the far horizon.
