What is Canada's fiscal policy stance?

Fiscal policy is modestly stimulative[1]. Carney's government is running a larger deficit than he inherited[2], but nothing near pandemic-level extremes[3]. The government has split the budget in two and says it will balance the operating books by 2028-29[4]. The PBO disputes this[5], suggesting the government has misclassified C$94 billion in operating expenses as capital investment[6].
