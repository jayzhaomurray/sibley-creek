How close is Canadian inflation to the 2% target?

Headline CPI sits at 2.3%[1] — a hair above target, with food and energy now carrying the overshoot that shelter used to. Core-trim and core-median, the measures the Bank of Canada actually reacts to, are back at 2.2-2.3%[2]. And consumer and firm expectations have moderated together: the anchor is holding while the composition rotates.

02 · Preferred cores

Core measures held near target as headline reaccelerated.

Core-trim and core-median, Y/Y
Core-trim eased to 2.2% in March[9] and core-median held at 2.3%[10] — both within a tenth of the 2% target[11] and both lower than they were three months ago. The headline reacceleration hasn't bled into the core measures, which is where the signal the Bank of Canada reads sits. Core-common, once the anchor measure, has been deprioritized in the policy frame.

04 · Sub-aggregates

Composition flipped: food and energy now carry the overshoot, not shelter.

Shelter, services, goods, food, energy, Y/Y
Shelter inflation, the engine of the 2023-24 overshoot[19], has cooled to 1.7%[20] — below the 2% target[21] for a third straight month[22]. Services run at 2.5%[23] and goods at 2.1%[24], both well-behaved. The pressure has rotated to food at 4.0%[25] and energy at 3.9%[26], the two components the Bank of Canada is most willing to look through. The overshoot has migrated, not vanished.

05 · Expectations

Expectations kept moderating as the headline jumped.

Consumer and firm inflation expectations · Q1 2026 (CSCE / BOS)
Five-year consumer inflation expectations in the CSCE eased to 3.0% in Q1[27], down from a recent-cycle peak of 3.7% in mid-2025[28]. On the firm side, the share of businesses expecting inflation above 3%[29] has collapsed to 11%[30], from around 40%[31] as recently as early 2024[32]. Both anchors held while the headline reaccelerated — which is why the Bank of Canada can tolerate a brief blip above 2%[33] without flinching on the rate path.
Bank of Canada Canadian Survey of Consumer Expectations and Business Outlook Survey, Q1 2026.
