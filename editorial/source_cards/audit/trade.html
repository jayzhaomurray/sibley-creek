US export share of total goods exports
The US absorbed 66.1% of Canadian goods exports in March 2026[22], down from 74.4% in March 2025[23] and a 2024 calendar average near 76.3%[24]. Total exports rose to a record $68.8 billion[25] over the same window, so the share decline reflects geographic redirection rather than an export contraction. The USMCA review is the visible event; the Section 232 stack is the binding exposure.

Tariff state: US trade actions affecting Canada · Current as of May 11, 2026
Section 232 rates of 50% on steel and aluminum[27] have been in force since June 2025[28]; copper joined the stack in April 2026[29] under the April 2 proclamation[30]. Autos and parts carry 25%[31], and a 10% Section 232 line on softwood lumber[32] stacks atop AD/CVD duties for an effective burden near 45%[33]. A recent Commerce Department preliminary review proposes a modest cut to the softwood AD/CVD components, pending final determinations later this year. The July 1 USMCA joint review[34] is the visible event; the sector-targeted Section 232 stack is the binding exposure underneath it.
