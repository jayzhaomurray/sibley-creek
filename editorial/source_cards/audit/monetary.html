What is Canada's monetary policy stance?

On hold. The Bank of Canada has stayed at 2.25%[1] through four straight decisions[2], sitting at the floor of its 2.25 to 3.25% neutral range[3]. Activity is softening at the same time the Iran-war oil shock is feeding energy inflation. The call hinges on persistence — how long the Strait of Hormuz stays closed, and whether the shock seeps into expectations.

04 · Asset composition

Government bonds anchor the post-QT balance sheet.

BoC asset composition
The asset side has settled into a shape defined by what's left of the Government Bond Purchase Programme. Government of Canada bonds account for $154.6 billion of $219.0 billion in total assets[18] — about 71%[19] — and the total has held near that level for roughly eighteen months. The overnight rate is now the only active policy lever.

BoC liability composition
Banknotes carry $122.5 billion of $219.0 billion in total liabilities[22] — about 56%[23] — drifting up only $30 billion over six years[24] as a passive function of currency demand.
