What is Canada's monetary policy stance?

On hold, with the pressure pointing up. The Bank of Canada has held at 2.25%[1], the floor of its neutral range[3], through seven straight decisions[2], but says upside risks to inflation have increased[4]. Bond markets lean the same way: the 2-year GoC yield is 3.27%[5], 102 bps above the overnight rate[6].

Seven holds in[9], the Bank now sees inflation risks tilting up[10].

Overnight rate · Sep 2, 2026 (rate decision)

03 · Market path

The 2-year yield has pulled a full point above the policy rate[22].

2y GoC vs overnight
The 2-year GoC yield closed at 3.27% on October 1[21], 102 bps above[23] the 2.25% overnight target[24]: a bond market leaning toward a higher policy rate. Before this September the gap had not been that wide since October 2022[25], during the hiking cycle; it was 17 bps on October 30, 2025[26], the day after the Bank's last cut. The yield reached 3.40% on September 23[27], its highest close since July 2024[28].
Bank of Canada Valet (2-year benchmark yield; overnight rate target).

05 · Liability composition

Settlement balances have jumped above the Bank's operating range.

BoC liability composition
Settlement balances rose $20.4 billion to $83.4 billion in the two weeks to September 30[34], above the $50 to 70 billion operating range[35] that held them at $63.1 billion on September 16[36]. The Canadian Overnight Repo Rate Average, a measure of overnight funding costs, has been 3 to 6 bps above the 2.25% target every day since September 1[37], after sitting exactly on target in late August[38].
