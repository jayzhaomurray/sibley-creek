## INTERPRETATION (take → mechanism → land; 2-4 sentences; 40-70 target, 95 cap)

The government's fiscal anchor is to balance day-to-day operating spending by
2028-29, and on its own books it gets there — a small operating surplus that
year, growing through 2030-31. That promise turns on what counts as capital,
which the government borrows to fund and keeps off the operating side: corporate
tax credits, investment tax credits and operating subsidies are all booked
there. Re-running Budget 2025 on a stricter, internationally standard definition,
the Parliamentary Budget Officer (PBO) moved roughly $94 billion of that back to
operating, and on the narrower line the operating books never reach balance. As
of May 2026 the government has not published the definitions it used — leaving
the anchor met only on a capital line the PBO says it cannot verify.

(Word count: 118 — OVER the 95 cap. See trimmed version below; this longer
form is kept only as the reference for what got cut.)

The government's fiscal anchor is a balanced operating budget by 2028-29; on

### The one correction (mechanical, auto-applied)

**"corporate tax credits" → "corporate tax breaks."**

The card category is "corporate income-tax **expenditures**." A tax expenditure is the
broad class — deductions, exclusions, preferential rates, deferrals, AND credits. "Tax
credits" is a strict subset, so "corporate tax credits" both narrows the category
incorrectly and collides awkwardly with the very next item in the list ("investment
tax credits"), reading as if the two are different flavours of the same thing. "Tax
breaks" is the correct broad plain-English render of "tax expenditures" and is the
defensible form. Note this is exactly the form the LIVE plate-2 copy already uses
(`src/pages/fiscal.astro` line 88: "strip corporate tax breaks, investment tax credits,
and operating subsidies"). Adopt it for consistency.

Corrected shipped sentence:
> That promise turns on what counts as capital — the half it funds by borrowing — and
> corporate tax breaks, investment tax credits and operating subsidies are all booked there.
