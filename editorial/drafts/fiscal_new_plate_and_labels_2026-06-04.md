## Surface fit (Gate 3 — JOB 3, the five-label SET)

Judging the labels as a nav set, not one at a time. The plate index is a scan surface — labels must be parallel, mutually distinct, and non-colliding.

**SET VERDICT: PASS on four, FLAG the plate-1/plate-2 collision.**

- Plate 1 **Operating vs capital** / Plate 2 **Whose capital?** — COLLISION. Read as a set, these two adjacent labels both foreground "capital" and the reader scanning the index sees "Operating vs capital" then "Whose capital?" back to back. The plate-1 label says the plate shows the operating/capital split; the plate-2 label asks whose capital definition is right. That's coherent as an argument but the two labels look like a near-duplicate pair in a vertical index, and a scanner can't tell from the labels alone which plate is the plan and which is the dispute. Worse, plate-1 ("Operating vs capital") describes a TWO-PANEL balance decomposition; the word "capital" doing double duty across two adjacent labels muddies which plate owns the operating/capital decomposition.
  - **Recommended fix:** keep plate-1 closer to what its two-panel chart literally shows and reserve the "capital definition" language for plate-2 alone. Plate-1 → **Operating balance** (the plan plate; names the metric the two-panel foregrounds) or keep the existing **Balance**. Plate-2 → the dispute. With plate-1 as "Operating balance," plate-2's **Whose capital?** stops colliding and reads as the distinct follow-on it is.
- Plate 2 **Whose capital?** — the question form breaks the noun-phrase parallel of the set (every other label is a noun phrase). On its own that's a defensible single exception for the one plate that IS a dispute, and it's register-fit (not cute for this readership). But combined with the collision above it's carrying too much. If plate-1 becomes "Operating balance," I'd take the draft's own fallback **Capital, disputed** for plate-2 — keeps the noun-phrase parallel across all five AND resolves the collision (one label = the metric, one label = the dispute over it). Recommended set: **Operating balance / Capital, disputed / Revenue vs spending / Debt/GDP / Issuance by instrument** — five parallel-ish noun phrases, no two colliding, each names its plate's actual content.
- Plate 3 **Revenue vs spending** — PASS. Parallel, distinct, matches the plate's own prose ("program spending").
- Plate 4 **Debt/GDP** — PASS. Conventional, exact, reads as a ratio.
- Plate 5 **Issuance by instrument** — PASS. Names the actual cut; "mix" was vague.

Not a Gate-3 cut to force the relabel — labels are an editorial-director call and this surface fit review IS that call. Recommending the **Operating balance / Capital, disputed** set; flagging the as-drafted "Operating vs capital / Whose capital?" pair as a scan-collision to resolve before wiring.
