## Flagged issues

### F1. CONTRADICTED: Panel 2 callout prior-month trim/median values + event blurb "held"

The writer's Panel 2 callout (Lines 140-142) asserts:
- Core-trim 2.2% (down from 2.3% prior)
- Core-median 2.3% (down from 2.4% prior)

And the event blurb (Lines 242) says: "Core-trim held at 2.2% and
core-median at 2.3%". These are mutually contradictory in the same draft
(the callout says "down from", the event blurb says "held").

Cross-checking against the BoC CPI page (re-fetched 2026-05-11):
- February 2026 trim Y/Y = 2.3%
- February 2026 median Y/Y = 2.3%

So the correct prior-period reading is:
- Trim: 2.3% Feb -> 2.2% Mar (DOWN one tenth)
- Median: 2.3% Feb -> 2.3% Mar (HELD)

The Panel 2 callout is correct on trim ("down from 2.3% prior") but
WRONG on median ("down from 2.4% prior" -- BoC page shows 2.3%, not
2.4%). The event blurb is correct on median ("held at 2.3%") but WRONG
on trim ("held at 2.2%" -- it actually ticked down from 2.3%).

The writer self-flagged this for the fact-checker (Line 143-144):
"specific February value to be confirmed by fact-checker against the
BoC CPI page". Verdict: CONTRADICTED on both lines. Writer correction
required before publication. Recommended fix:
- Panel 2 callout: "Trim ticked down from 2.3% to 2.2%; median held at
  2.3%."
- Event blurb: "Core-trim ticked down to 2.2% from 2.3%, core-median
  held at 2.3%; both at or fractionally above the 2% target."

### F2. Minor: Panel 1 callout phrasing "0.1pp below consensus[c]"

The Panel 1 callout (Line 135) renders the surprise as "0.1pp below
consensus[c]". W3-R2 cross-cutting note 1 explicitly recommends
"below the pre-print consensus of around 2.5%" with a date stamp,
rather than a precise -0.1pp surprise, until the Big-Six aggregation
pipeline is built. The writer's event blurb (Line 241) uses the
recommended fuzzy framing ("0.1pp below the pre-print consensus of
around 2.5%"); the Panel 1 callout uses the tighter "-0.1pp" phrasing.
Both are supported by W3-R2 Panel 1 (which itself states the surprise
as -0.1pp in its top-of-Panel-1 table), but the W3-R2 capture-honesty
guidance favors the fuzzy framing. Style-editor may prefer to soften
the callout phrasing to match the event blurb; not strictly a fact
error. Style discretion.

### F3. CONTRADICTED: Panel 3 callout "middle band smallest"

The Panel 3 callout (Line 167) says: "The middle band is the smallest
of the three." Numerical reading: 44.0% below 1%, 28.8% in 1-3%, 27.2%
above 3%. The smallest band is "above 3%" at 27.2%, NOT the middle
band (28.8%). The middle band is the SECOND-smallest -- between the
top (44%) and the smallest (27%).

This contradicts what W3-R2 Panel 3 actually says (Line 327 in W3-R2):
"Only ~29% sits in the 1-3% band -- the smallest of the three tiers."
W3-R2 is also wrong on this arithmetic check.

Re-checking: 44.0% below 1%, 28.8% in 1-3%, 27.2% above 3%. 27.2 < 28.8
< 44.0. So the smallest tier is above-3% at 27.2%, the second smallest
is the 1-3% middle band at 28.8%.

Reasonable polish: the middle band is "near-tied for smallest" or
"the second smallest band, very close in size to the above-3% tier" --
this would be defensible. As written, "smallest of the three" is
incorrect by 1.6 percentage points.

The page lede (Line 53) is also affected: "a wide distribution, not a
clustered one" -- this is correct overall, but the supporting framing
that the middle band is small is mathematically inaccurate.

Writer rework required.

### F4. Note: Panel 6 USDCAD value rounded

The Panel 6 callout (Line 221) shows USDCAD 1.36 with "loonie roughly
1.25% stronger Y/Y". W3-R2 Panel 6 records spot 1.3575 at 2026-05-01
(month-end-last). 1.3575 rounds to 1.36. Writer also flagged this
explicitly in flag #4 (Line 399-404). Acceptable rounding; cross-check
against sections.ts (USDCAD 1.378 on 2026-05-09) shows the spot has
moved since the W3-R2 capture date. Not a draft error; the basics page
is anchored to the inflation-print reference month (March 2026) and the
Panel 6 callout will refresh on subsequent updates per W3-R2 vintage
discipline. NOTE only.

### F5. Note: TD-cited "grocery inflation 4.4%" vs draft's "food 4.0%"

The writer's lede uses "food at 4.0%" (Line 50) and Panel 4 callout
"Food 4.0% Y/Y" (Line 186). W3-R2 Panel 4 reconciles: 4.0% is the
all-food aggregate (StatCan v41690974), while TD's "4.4%" is food
from stores (a sub-aggregate). Both are correct at different
aggregation levels. The writer is consistent on all-food. StatCan
Daily 2026-04-20 (re-fetched) cites "Food from stores 4.4%" -- which
matches TD's figure but is not what the writer is anchoring to.
Acceptable. NOTE only.
